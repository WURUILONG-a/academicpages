I received my **B.E. degree in Automation** from **Qilu University of Technology** in 2023.  
My research focuses on **digital twin technology**, **intelligent fault diagnosis**, and **control system design** for industrial and hydraulic systems.  
I am passionate about integrating modeling, simulation, and real-time monitoring to enhance the reliability and intelligence of complex electromechanical equipment!

Research Content / 研究内容
======
My research focuses on **digital twin–based intelligent fault diagnosis for hydraulic robots**.  
The goal is to establish high-fidelity digital twin models that integrate mechanical structure, hydraulic dynamics, and sensor data to achieve accurate fault identification, condition monitoring, and performance evaluation.  
By combining data-driven methods with physical modeling, I aim to enhance the system’s fault tolerance, predictive capability, and overall operational reliability.  
This research contributes to the development of intelligent maintenance and autonomous decision-making technologies for next-generation hydraulic robotic systems.
